This was the first of two sessions at the October AI/ML Engineering meetup on local and sovereign AI. The question: if you use Claude Code with a frontier model every day, what does it take to get real work done with a coding agent running entirely on your own laptop?

The deck walks through it in order:

- **The model.** Why a 27B model when giants are a click away, and what makes Qwen3.8-27B stand out.
- **The runtime.** Two engines compared: LM Studio and MTPLX.
- **Pi as a coding environment.** How it sits next to Claude Code, and what it's like to drive.
- **Making it safe.** Claude Code's safety in four layers, and a host sandbox for Pi.
- **Prompting a smaller model.** Tests as the steering wheel (red, then green), living within a smaller context window, and the prompt habits that suit a 27B.
- **Getting started.** A cheat sheet from zero to a working local agent, plus my settings.

The slides work in any browser: arrow keys to move, G to see every slide, N for speaker notes. New to this? Start with slide 02, the cheat sheet.

The workshop's companion piece is [COLD STORAGE](/projects/cold-storage/), a text adventure written entirely by the local model.
