For the last couple of years I've been giving talks and workshops on AI engineering, hosting meetups, and building things in public: decks, workshop starters, a Mac dictation app, a haunted text adventure written by a model on my laptop. Most of it ended up as a LinkedIn post that scrolled away within a week.

This site is the permanent home for that work. Every talk gets a page with its slides and code. Every project gets a page with what it is and why I built it. And in between, I'll write up experiments and findings as I go: what I tried, what happened, and what surprised me. Small and frequent beats polished and rare.

## Where to start

- [Inside Voice](/inside-voice/), free dictation for the Mac where nothing you say leaves your computer.
- [Pi on a local model](/talks/pi-on-a-local-model/), my field guide to running a coding agent entirely on a laptop.
- [Monster Scanner](/talks/monster-scanner/), a vision-language model on an iPhone, deployed as a Halloween party game.

If you'd like to follow along, there's an [RSS feed](/index.xml). And if something here is useful, wrong, or gives you an idea, tell me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/billmcintyre/).
